RELEASE PHILOSOPHY

The first public RISU Institute surface should feel like a serious research
institution and standards artifact, not a startup landing page. Do not add
marketing copy, stock art, gradients, dashboards, signup flows, analytics,
frameworks, or invented institutional claims.

The site must state only what is already true:
- `appeal` is experimental.
- consumer-side zero-provider-mapping recognition has been demonstrated under
  supported bindings.
- independent native publication by an external publisher has NOT yet been
  demonstrated.
- the current first public binding is RFC 8288.
- the canonical URI is controlled by RISU Institute.
- discovery does not imply authorization, entitlement, trust, freshness,
  invocation success, or universal workflow semantics.
