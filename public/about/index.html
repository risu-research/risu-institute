About RISU Institute

An independent research initiative of RISU LLC, based in Washington, DC.

What we study

RISU Institute studies how software preserves meaning, evidence and safeguards as information moves between systems. Its research spans automated decision-making, software standards, legal and administrative records, and empirical analysis of public data.

The institute publishes technical papers, develops research software and prepares datasets that researchers can use in their own work.

Research methods

Projects begin with a specific question about a system, a rule or a record. The research draws on formal methods, original source material and experiments with actual software. Counterexamples and independent comparisons help determine where a result applies and which assumptions it depends on.

Public research records connect findings to their supporting sources, code and evaluation results. The research program describes the technical projects in more detail.

Publications and evidence

The publication record includes technical notes, conference papers and presentations. Research software is available through the public workbench and archived releases. Research data includes the RISUAP Colorado series published in Harvard Dataverse.

Organization

RISU Institute is a research initiative of RISU LLC. Public software and experimental records are maintained through the RISU Research GitHub organization.
