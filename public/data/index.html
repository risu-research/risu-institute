RISUAP Colorado Administrative Event Series, 2018–2026

RISU Institute · 25 November 2026

The Colorado series traces recorded business registry activity from 2018 through 2026. RISUAP prepared seven aggregate research tables from 10,089,541 transaction rows and two observed editions of the business registry. The tables support longitudinal analysis, comparisons of administrative counting units, and assessments of source coverage and registry joins.

Findings from the series

Counting units

15,660 business entity IDs, one transaction ID

On 9 October 2024, Colorado's Business Entity Transaction History records 15,660 entries under the description Court Order. Every entry refers to a different business entity ID; all carry the same native transaction ID.

The distinction extends beyond a single date. Among 1,991 received-date and description groups with at least 1,000 source rows, 101 groups exhibit a row-count and transaction-ID-count difference. These groups contain 562,750 rows and 339,312 distinct transaction IDs summed within groups. Twenty-two registered-agent-change groups and one Court Order group account for 99.9% of the resulting 223,438-count gap.

Registry joins

The registry edition changes the population retained by a join

The transaction history contains 2,635,090 distinct business entity IDs. An inner join to the registry edition captured on 17 November 2026 would retain 2,141,239 of them. Comparing the two source editions makes the effect of registry selection visible.

Research tables

Sources and reproducibility

The underlying government records are published by the Colorado Secretary of State through the Colorado Information Marketplace. RISUAP prepared the aggregate tables from documented source captures.

  • Business Entity Transaction History
  • Business Entities in Colorado

The deposit includes a canonical CSV archive, codebook, methods, coverage documentation, source and reuse information, SHA-256 checksums and two offline Python scripts. The scripts verify the distributed files and reproduce the main numerical results.
