Overview

The series organizes Colorado business registry transactions into monthly and daily counts by transaction type, alongside measures of source coverage, identifier multiplicity, master-record editions and join coverage. The source selection spans 1 January 2018 through 16 November 2026; the latest received date observed in the selected records is 9 November 2026.

Researchers can use the tables to study changes in recorded administrative activity over time, compare counting units, and assess how source editions and identifier relationships affect empirical measures. The release includes a data guide, codebook, methods, source and reuse information, example queries, and a verification script.
