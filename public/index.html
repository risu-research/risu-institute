Research for consequential machine action.

RISU studies what must remain true as machine-readable meaning crosses evidence, interfaces, and action boundaries. The work turns those distinctions into bounded definitions, executable checks, and inspectable public records.

Research architecture

Three bounded questions, one consequence layer.

01 / MEANING

What does the machine claim mean?

Semantic identity and preservation make meaning explicit before another system relies on it.

02 / EVIDENCE

What justifies reliance?

Evidence qualification and transport separate an observation from the stronger premise a downstream action may consume.

03 / ACTION

What is fixed at the action cut?

Closure and operativity ask whether the distinctions that matter to the consequence have actually been established and still govern the effect.

Selected research

A research program built around failure at boundaries.

Each project names a narrow failure mode, states a bounded claim, and keeps the formal, empirical, and operational layers separate.
