Six bounded failure modes · 2026

Missing. Invented. Lost. Unwarranted. Laundered. Unresolved.

What must remain explicit when software exposes, translates, relies on, or retires machine-readable state?

RISU isolates small failure modes where automated systems lose control of meaning or consequence: a shared semantic may be absent; translation may invent or discard meaning; a negative premise may outrun its evidence; evidentiary status may strengthen in transit; or a retired agent may leave live operational consequences behind.

Latest artifact

Retirement is not closure.

UNRESOLVED · Deterministic verifier · 2026

Bounded Agent Closure

After an autonomous agent is disabled, what proves that the consequences attributable to it are actually over?

Bounded Agent Closure separates administrative retirement from operational closure. Its verifier follows evidence-backed residual consequences transitively across authority, execution, commitments, and operational state, checks postconditions and source stability, and requires convergent terminal observations before issuing a bounded CLOSED certificate.
