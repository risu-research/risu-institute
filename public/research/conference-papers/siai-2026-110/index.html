The 2024 audit and its governing contract

MCNA Dental's original Louisiana contract required a signed confirmation within 15 days of an oral appeal. Amendment 2 removed that obligation in 2021. Louisiana's 2025 review of the plan's 2024 operations nevertheless found written follow-up instructions in its Adult Denture and EPSDT member handbooks.

The same review found two internal policies counting the state fair hearing period from the initial adverse-benefit notice. The amended contract specified 120 calendar days after receipt of the plan's appeal-resolution notice. Six elements of the grievance-and-appeal standard were rated unmet.

Corrections linked to audit items and contract clauses

For continued benefits, 42 CFR § 438.420(c) listed three ending events. The original Louisiana contract included a fourth event when the authorized service period or limit was reached, which Policy 13.209 omitted. Section 438.420(b)(4) addressed initial eligibility, a separate issue documented in the source comparison.

Retrieval results across two Nevada audits

In three frozen tests, procedural-stage-filtered TF–IDF ranked the author-assigned requirement first for 8 of 8 SilverSummit action queries, 7 of 8 SilverSummit finding queries and 3 of 7 Molina finding queries. BM25 returned 7 of 8, 5 of 8 and 3 of 7 on the same tasks. The cards and target IDs were prepared from the cited agency reports.

Removing an assigned requirement sometimes pushed a neighboring clause to the top with a larger score gap. A score-gap rule chosen on the first task accepted seven correct results and withheld all eight removed-source cases. Applied unchanged to the other two tasks, it withheld every result. The frozen datasets, predictions and reproduction scripts record the comparisons.

Additional contract and handbook evidence

Subsequent source work indexed the original Louisiana contract and Amendments 1–13 against the federal requirements and 2024 audit findings. The public crosswalk records the contract clauses and audit pages for the oral-appeal, hearing-deadline and continued-benefits issues.

Two MCNA handbooks dated 12 August 2025 permit appeals by telephone. Their acknowledgment sections nevertheless begin “After we receive your appeal in writing.” The handbook evidence record preserves the editions, PDF pages and SHA-256 fingerprints. The earlier handbook PDFs cited by the auditor and an approved corrective-action plan were unavailable in the reviewed public sources.

On 12 March 2026, Louisiana's health department assessed $30,000 across six compliance standards with unmet elements, including grievances and appeals. The notice required completion records after corrective-action-plan approval; it did not allocate the penalty among individual findings.
