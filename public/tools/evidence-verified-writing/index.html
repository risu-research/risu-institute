Proof-carrying research-writing prototype · October 2026

Evidence-Verified Research Writing

A manuscript claim can change while its citations stay in place. This prototype binds the exact wording and structured claim meaning to exact evidence, issues a content-addressed certificate, and independently checks that certificate before the workflow can continue.

Prototype boundary Preloaded illustrative evidence. Deterministic claim contracts. Two separate browser implementations for certificate production and verification. No live search or model inference.
What changed

The first prototype showed revision-aware evidence checking. This version makes the decision portable and independently checkable. The producer emits a certificate over the claim, evidence set, scope, counts and verdict. A separate checker rehashes the supplied bytes and recomputes the decision from the evidence.

Technical relationship to RISU

    

      
The manuscript workflow has its own claim model. RISU contributes the assurance pattern.

      
The prototype carries over explicit semantic boundaries, content-addressed evidence, independent checking and mutation-based qualification from existing RISU assurance work. It applies those mechanisms to a new research-writing object: the relationship between a versioned manuscript claim and the evidence that licenses it.
