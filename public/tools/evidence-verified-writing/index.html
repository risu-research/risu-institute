Research-writing prototype · October 2026

Evidence-Verified Research Writing

A manuscript claim can change while its citations stay in place. This prototype checks whether the evidence still supports the exact current wording and stops the workflow when support becomes contradictory, incomplete, or stale.

Prototype boundary Preloaded illustrative evidence. Deterministic browser logic. No live search, no model inference, and no claim of production deployment.
What this demonstrates

A research-writing agent should preserve more than a bibliography. For each material sentence it should retain the evidence used, the scope of that evidence, and the manuscript version the evidence justified. When the sentence changes, the binding is rechecked before the workflow continues.

How this relates to RISU

The product question comes first. Existing assurance work supplies the checking discipline.

This prototype reuses several patterns already exercised in RISU research software: explicit claim boundaries, content-addressed records, exact identity checks, fail-closed outcomes, and human escalation when evidence is insufficient. The manuscript workflow is a new application surface.
