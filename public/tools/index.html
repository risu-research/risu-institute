Research instruments

Selected RISU projects include public instruments for examining executable semantics, recorded evidence, and bounded verification results. The instruments expose what a research claim depends on without enlarging the scope stated by the underlying artifact.

Local first · Frozen v0.5.0 · 2026

Consequence Closure Inspector

What can still change the consequence of this action, and what declared step can close the gap?

The Inspector recomputes supported bounded analyses locally and presents consequence status, inclusion minimal obligation families, materiality and removal witnesses, declared Routes, source lineage, and assurance qualifications. The hosted application serves the frozen browser files without adding a semantic backend.
