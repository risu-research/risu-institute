Research program

From meaning to consequence.

RISU studies how machine-readable claims acquire operational force as they move from semantic identity, through evidence and reliance, to action. Each project remains bounded to its own formal and empirical record.

Research disciplineClaims are scoped before they are generalized. Formal consequence, empirical observation, source preservation, and operative evidence remain separate unless a specific artifact establishes the link.

Consequence assurance · RISU Technical Note 2026-03

Consequence Closure

At the moment a machine acts, is the specified consequence already determined by the evidence and operative state?

Consequence Closure defines consequence determinacy at a declared action cut. Relational counterexamples preserve what remains material, a materiality hypergraph characterizes inclusion-minimal semantic obligation sets, and finite CEGIS either returns one such set or exposes a declared vocabulary boundary. Obligations remain separate from realizable Establishments and Routes.

01.3

Finality and closure · RISU Technical Note 2026-01

Bounded Agent Closure

After an autonomous agent is disabled, what proves that the consequences attributable to it have actually reached a terminal, stable state?

Bounded Agent Closure distinguishes administrative retirement from operational closure. Its deterministic verifier follows evidence-backed residual consequences transitively from the retiring root, evaluates disposition-specific postconditions, and requires stable convergence before issuing the bounded claim CLOSED.

Evidence qualification · executable reference profile

Negative Result Warrant

When a bounded query returns zero, what evidence is sufficient for a downstream operation to use that exact negative premise?

The profile makes a bounded negative query premise usable only after provider-reported evidence is sufficient, bound to the exact proposition, transported, and receiver-validated. Its recorded Algolia contrast separates an ordinary zero-result observation from a separately warranted zero.

Research thread 03

Semantic identity and preservation.

03.1

Projection assurance · RISU Technical Note 2026-04

Projection Assurance

When a source operation becomes an agent-facing tool, do the distinctions that determine its consequence survive the projection and still govern the effect?

Projection Assurance separates surface retention from consequence preservation. Correspondence licenses the mapping, discrimination tests whether consequence-distinct worlds remain distinguishable, operativity asks whether the distinction gates the effect, and exact realization and coverage remain separate claims.
