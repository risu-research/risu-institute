# G11 reset-boundary contract audit (2026-09-20)

Independent rerun of the pinned full-CPU 2018 parent/child Yosys models with Z3 4.13.3.0 found that retaining `dblfetch.v:350` `i_new_pc` no-consecutive-pulse restriction makes the target-free base model UNSAT by s2, even with every CPU assertion removed. Excluding assumptions, but not assertions, recovers a SAT base. Reinstating this restriction **at every state except s2** while keeping the original two-word ROM, real dblfetch and mpyop, full hierarchy, top-level assumptions and other assertions returns child SAT / parent strict UNSAT / parent old-address-assertion excluded SAT. Requiring the restriction at s2 makes all three UNSAT. Source-level candidate (FORMAL-only) is to guard the new-PC restriction with `!f_past_reset`; must independently rebuild model and prove whether it reproduces the matched triplet rather than treating SMT-level guard as source-level verification. Nothing here establishes an original unmodified formal run, new CPU functional defect, or novelty of reset-aware assumptions. Preserve initial reset contract and exact ROM unchanged.
